I am a student in Barrett, The Honors College at Arizona State University, studying computer science with an emphasis in cybersecurity. In addition to this, I am pursuing a minor in mathematics. I am passionate about solving real-world problems and protecting technology. I am especially interested in cryptography and cyber forensics. I love seeing how computer science and cybersecurity can make a meaningful impact. Through the Barrett College Fellows Undergraduate Research Program I will begin aiding with research in the Fall 2025 semester. I am very excited to begin this project in addition to continuing to build my technical skills and community involvement.

Beyond academics, I serve as Vice President of Finance for the Epsilon Zeta chapter of Alpha Epsilon Phi. I also volunteer for Hugh O'Brian Youth (HOBY) Leadership Arizona at their annual leadership seminar for rising high school juniors. This coming seminar (2025) I will be serving in the role of junior facilitator team lead. In addition to better connecting me to my community, these roles have deepended my leadership, organization, and teamwork skills.
